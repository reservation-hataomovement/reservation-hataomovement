## 🎯 Mission Statement

<blockquote align="center">
  <i>"Affirmative action should uplift those in genuine current need, regardless of lineage or background."</i>
</blockquote>

This initiative seeks to foster constructive, policy-driven public discourse regarding the modernization of reservation criteria in India. Our objective is to propose a framework where state support and affirmative action are allocated strictly based on **verifiable individual hardship, physical ability, and economic need**.

---

## 🏛️ Core Proposals

We advocate for amending the affirmative action framework so that reservation and state assistance are restructured around **4 primary categories**:

| Category | Description | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ♿ **Persons with Disabilities (PwD)** | Dedicated opportunities, equal access, and infrastructural support in education & employment. | Equal Physical Access |
| 👶 **Orphans & Unparented Youth** | Comprehensive institutional, educational, and developmental backing for children without family safety nets. | Social Protection |
| 🎖️ **Children of Fallen Service Members** | Securing educational and developmental support for dependents of martyrs (Armed Forces, Paramilitary, & Police). | Honoring Sacrifice |
| 💼 **Economically Weaker Sections (EWS)** | Assistance based strictly on documented income thresholds, applied uniformly across all citizens. | Universal Economic Need |

## 💡 Key Principles

* **⚖️ Universal Fairness:** Target measurable socioeconomic hardship rather than inherited identity.
* **🗣️ Constructive Dialogue:** Focus on legal frameworks, empirical economic data, and civic policy reform.
* **🤝 Inclusivity:** Ensure every citizen facing genuine economic or physical barriers receives fair state backing.

---

## 📜 Community Guidelines

To maintain a constructive and impactful environment, all contributors must adhere to the following:

- 📊 **Focus on Policy:** Keep discussions grounded in research, constitutional amendments, and economic data.
- 🤝 **Maintain Respect:** Avoid personal attacks, hostility, or discriminatory language toward any community or group.
- ⚖️ **Democratic Alignment:** Advocate through rational, legal, and peaceful processes.
